ESB P1 Reader

Flash an ESP32-C6 from your browser with ESPHome firmware that reads the P1 port of an ESB Networks smart meter and sends the data to Home Assistant.

Before you flash

  • Read the disclaimer. Use of the P1 port is subject to ESB Networks' conditions. A self-built device is not a certified product, and you fit one at your own risk.
  • This is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with or supported by ESB Networks.
  • This firmware is early. It has not yet been confirmed on a meter by this project.

What this firmware expects

  • An ESP32-C6 board (built for the ESP32-C6-DevKitC-1 with an ESP32-C6-WROOM-1 module, 4 MB flash or more).
  • The P1 data line (RJ12 pin 5) wired to GPIO6, with a pull-up resistor to 3.3 V.
  • The P1 port enabled in your ESB Networks online account.

A different board or pin needs its own build. See the README and the wiring notes.

Install

  1. Unplug the RJ12 cable from the meter. Do not power the board from USB and the meter at the same time.
  2. Connect the board to this computer with a USB data cable. On a DevKitC-1, use the port labelled USB, so the installer can ask for your Wi-Fi details afterwards.
  3. Press the button, pick the board's serial port, and choose to erase the device when asked.
Your browser cannot do this. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Firefox, Safari and phone browsers do not support Web Serial. This page must be opened over HTTPS for the installer to work.

If no port appears, try another cable (many are charge-only) or hold the board's BOOT button while plugging it in.

After flashing

  1. Give it your Wi-Fi details. The installer offers this when it finishes. If it does not, join the hotspot named esb-p1-reader-xxxxxx from your phone and enter them there. The ESP32-C6 uses 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only.
  2. Add it to Home Assistant. It appears under Settings, Devices & services as a discovered ESPHome device.
  3. Secure it. This shared firmware holds no secrets, so until you add your own, anyone on your network can read its sensors and update its firmware, and its setup hotspot has no password. Adopt it in the ESPHome dashboard and add an encryption key, as described in the README.
  4. Unplug USB, then connect the board to the meter's P1 port.
